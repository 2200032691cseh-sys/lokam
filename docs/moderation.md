# Moderation

Report content/profile with category, optional evidence and minimal identifiers. Rate-limit reporting. Keep the queue restricted, assign cases, record rationale and scope, notify as appropriate, support appeals and re-review by a separate moderator where available. Capture minimal restricted evidence, keep actions append-only, support suspension, removal and restoration, and limit administrator overrides. Handle impersonation, threats/harassment, spam and copyright with documented routes and evidence retention.

Before public launch identify jurisdictions and obtain appropriate review of privacy and intermediary obligations, copyright notice/removal, consent, age eligibility, appeals, retention, image and provider terms. Terms, privacy, community guidelines and credits go live after review. Record response ownership, escalation time and access scopes. Legal and moderation staffing decisions remain open.
