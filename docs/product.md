# Lokam — A world of your own

Lokam turns deliberately saved interests into a personal website and cross-domain discovery. The loop is discover → explore → collect → understand preferences → discover. One member can save a book, find its adaptation and explore a related place before anybody else joins.

Visitors browse authorized profiles, collections, catalogue and relationships. Members own libraries, collection and publication controls. Moderators review assigned cases. Administrators manage catalogue integrity, integrations and operations with MFA and audit.

The site at /u/<username>/ has exactly 20 rearrangeable, hideable page slots. Optional introduction appears as ordinary prose, followed by enabled navigation and selected content. Sample introduction is user-provided reference material. Use finite pages, optional onboarding, explicit preference signals and private defaults. Preserve the [requirements baseline](requirements.md) and [release backlog](../tasks/README.md).

## Acceptance by workflow

| Workflow | Evidence required |
|---|---|
| Landing, signup, verification, login, logout, recovery and onboarding | A fresh user can verify, skip optional steps, save a provider-backed item in one confirmation after selection, sign out and recover access. |
| Discover, global search, saved items and item detail | Search exact, partial, typo and cross-language cases; save or manually enter an item during provider outage; get an explained one-user cross-domain result and dismiss it. |
| Personal website | Exercise all 20 pages in their empty and populated states. Reorder, hide and publish; check the result as owner, anonymous visitor, unrelated member, approved follower and blocked account. |
| Followers, following, requests, notifications and blocked accounts | Approve and revoke follower access and verify dependent views stop exposing records. |
| Account, profile and privacy settings | Update optional profile information, preview access and invalidate old sessions when a security-sensitive credential changes. |
| Import, export and erasure | Retry a partially failed import without duplicates, export a documented machine-readable bundle and erase personal rows and files while preserving shared canonical records. |
| Help, About, terms, privacy, guidelines and credits | Publish reviewed support, rights and policy information; connect accessible reporting controls to it. |
| Reporting, moderator and admin | File a report, assign it, review scoped evidence, audit the decision and exercise appeal and administrator MFA. |
