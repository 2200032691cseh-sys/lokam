# Optional conversational profiles

Global and per-profile defaults are disabled. Opt-in selects sharing level and eligible records. Identify as AI in third person. Retrieve scoped authorized favourites, interests and collections; construct bounded context; call a provider-independent adapter; validate cited IDs and reauthorize before output. Decline unsupported claims and provide links. Prompt and profile text are untrusted data. Model has no tools, arbitrary actions or SQL. Isolate context by request, avoid histories and sensitive logs, enforce rate/token/time budgets and cancel or discard after revocation.

Benchmark Ollama and llama.cpp with a licensed small model for CPU latency, peak RAM, grounded citations, injection resistance and cross-user isolation. Keep disabled in production until hosting cost, data handling, quality and security are reviewed. Evaluate ASGI separately if streaming is justified.
